Why Traditional Menus Are Hurting Your Brand

A side-by-side look at how traditional dining stacks up against the FreeMenu standard.
Traditional Paper / Acrylic Menus
  • Expensive to Reprint: Changing just 2 dish prices costs thousands of rupees in fresh booklet printing.
  • Dirty & Unhygienic: Oily fingers, food sauce drops, and torn plastic leave a cheap first impression on guests.
  • Zero Dish Visualization: Guests have to guess portion sizes and appearance from plain black text.
  • Out-of-Stock Embarrassment: Waiters have to verbally apologize when a dish is finished during rush hours.
The FreeMenu Smart QR Standard
  • Instant Edits Anytime: Update prices, mark dishes out of stock, or launch specials in 5 seconds from your phone.
  • Premium Table Presence: Guests scan a sleek acrylic/wooden QR stand with their smartphone camera.
  • Sensational Visuals & 3D AR: Showcase crisp food photos, chef notes, and AR models right on the dining table.
  • Multi-Portion Clarity: Clear pricing columns for Full, Half, and Quarter portions without clutter.
