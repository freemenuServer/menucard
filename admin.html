Plans & Pricing

Control every plan shown on /owner/plans.html. Changes are stored in Supabase and do not require editing the pricing page code.

   
 
 
Plans are loaded from the Supabase plans table. Current plans include Free Starter, Growth (Photos), Signature 3D, Full 3D Experience, Cart Menu, and MenuOne once the setup SQL has been run. Setup fees, limits, feature text, badges, buttons, recommendation status, active status and display order are controlled here.
