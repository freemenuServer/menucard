Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about FreeMenu

How long does it take to get our restaurant menu live?
Less than 15 minutes! Once you register, you can immediately input your dishes or upload your menu using our CSV bulk importer, download your unique tabletop QR code, and start serving.
Do guests need to download any application to view the menu or AR models?
Zero app downloads are required. Diners simply point their default smartphone camera at the QR stand, and the menu opens instantly in their browser with high-speed Cloudflare edge caching.
Can we update dish prices during active operating hours?
Yes, changes reflect in real-time. If you adjust a price, toggle an item out-of-stock, or add a daily special, guests will see the updated menu the moment they refresh.
