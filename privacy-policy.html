Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026 • Effective Date: Immediate

Welcome to FreeMenu (referred to as "FreeMenu", "we", "our", or "us", accessible via freemenu.in). We provide interactive digital QR restaurant menus, multimedia management, and augmented reality (AR) dining experiences.

Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how we process and protect that information, and your rights when you visit our website, view digital restaurant menus, or use our cloud storage and interactive AR viewers.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in two primary categories:

  • Information from Restaurant Owners & Super Admins: Account credentials (such as username, registered phone number, email address), business name, contact information, menu items, prices, uploaded images, and subscription preferences.
  • Information from Menu Visitors (Diners): When a guest scans a restaurant QR code and accesses a digital menu, we do not require personal sign-ups or profile creation. However, standard automated web traffic data is logged (including browser type, operating system, anonymous device identifiers, IP address, and referring URLs).

2. Camera Access and Augmented Reality (AR) Features

Zero Camera Recording Policy: FreeMenu utilizes WebXR and HTML5 camera APIs strictly to anchor interactive 3D models and video projections onto real-world surfaces (such as dining tables).

When you activate our AR features, your device prompts for camera permissions. The camera stream is processed in real time entirely on your local device. FreeMenu does not capture, store, transmit, or record any images, faces, video feeds, or biometric data from your camera feed to our servers.

3. Cookies and Advertising (Google AdSense)

FreeMenu uses third-party advertising services, primarily Google AdSense, to serve advertisements on select free-tier digital restaurant menus.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on FreeMenu.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to FreeMenu and other websites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or via aboutads.info.

4. Cloud Infrastructure and Data Storage

We work with enterprise-grade cloud providers to deliver fast and reliable services:

  • Supabase: Used for secure database storage, authentication, and session handling.
  • Cloudflare (CDN & R2 Object Storage): Used to deliver cached menu assets, media files, and ensure global DDoS protection and secure asset delivery.

All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is protected using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

5. How We Use Your Information

Collected data is used solely for:

  • Displaying live restaurant menus, prices, and categories accurately.
  • Maintaining administrative security and authenticating restaurant owners.
  • Monetizing free-tier public menus via compliant advertising slots.
  • Improving platform performance, eliminating bugs, and mitigating malicious traffic.

6. Sharing of Personal Data

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal identification information to third parties. We may disclose data only if required by law or in good faith to protect our legal rights, property, or customer safety.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Restaurant owners retain full control over their uploaded menus and media. If you wish to delete your restaurant account, wipe uploaded assets from Cloudflare R2, or update your registered email/phone credentials, you may do so through your dashboard or by submitting a request to our administrative team.

8. Children's Privacy

FreeMenu does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Our digital menus and AR viewers are intended for general public audiences.
